Get straight to playing

Three steps. You're in.

Joining for the first time? Add NimersMC to your Minecraft Java server list.

STEP 01

Open Minecraft Java

Launch Java Edition and choose Multiplayer from the main menu.

STEP 02

Add the server

Click Add Server. Enter nimermc.net as the server address.

STEP 03

Make yourself at home

Save the server, click Join Server, and start your next adventure.

Events & rewardsKOTH, bosses, piñatas, and crate drops
  • KOTH: Capture the zone and hold it for rewards. Use /warp koth.
  • Bosses: Team up, defeat the boss, and collect its loot.
  • Piñata parties: Join the crowd and collect rewards together.
  • Drops & CrazyEnvoys: Race for the crates and claim event loot.

Event times and announcements are posted on our Discord.
